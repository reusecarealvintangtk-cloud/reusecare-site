Before you order.

Product-specific answers for your sourcing review.

What material is used?

PUL is the specified material. The full construction, including the skin-contact and absorbent layers, is confirmed for the selected style.

What size is available?

The listed option is one size. Ask for the exact dimensions and a sample before confirming your order.

How many nursing pads come in a pack?

Pack quantities vary by product. Tell us how many pieces or pairs you want in each set.

Can I customize the packaging?

Yes. Share your intended pack quantity, packaging format and artwork so the requirements can be reviewed.

Plan the layer stack and retail pack together.

A nursing-pad quotation should identify the contact layer, absorbent core, waterproof layer, finished dimensions and whether the selling unit is a piece, pair or set.
