Buyer sample guide

How to Evaluate a PUL Fabric Sample Before Buying 5 Metres

A small swatch is a useful first filter. Check the construction, feel and behavior against your own product brief, write down the method and acceptance rule, then decide whether a 5 metre trial is justified.

Prepared by ReuseCareBuyer evaluation worksheetUpdated November 10, 20268 minute read
Start with a traceable sample

The current published reference is a 100% polyester base fabric with a laminated TPU waterproof membrane. Published commercial reference points are 150 cm width, 160 g/m² and a 5 metre minimum order; confirm the exact specification for the selected color, print and batch before purchase.

1. Confirm what the swatch is

Write the supplier’s material description on the sample record before testing. A PUL label alone does not identify the base textile, membrane, finished weight, width, color or lot. The currently published reference described above is a polyester-base/TPU-membrane construction. Ask the supplier to confirm that the sample in your hand matches the proposed 5 metre material and to identify its sample or batch reference.

If your brief calls for 95% polyester and 5% elastane with a PU layer, record that as a separate requested construction. It differs from the current 100% polyester-base plus TPU reference in both base-fiber composition and the named membrane material. Request and evaluate a sample made to the requested construction; do not treat results from the current reference as proof that the alternative is equivalent.

See the PUL fabric range, the PUL vs TPU guide and the lamination process overview for context. Confirm batch-specific details directly before placing an order.

2. Compare handfeel, thickness and weight

Inspect both faces under consistent lighting. Note softness, surface texture, drape, stiffness, noise, visible defects and whether the laminate feels evenly bonded. Compare samples at the same temperature and with the same handling. If thickness matters to sewing or finished-product bulk, state how and where you will measure it; do not infer thickness from the published g/m² value.

For weight, agree on a measurement approach and sample conditioning, then record the measured value and locations. The published 160 g/m² is a commercial reference, not a measurement of every swatch or a tolerance statement. Ask which batch-specific value and allowable variation apply to the material offered.

3. Check lengthwise and crosswise stretch separately

Mark a known gauge length along the fabric length (warp direction, if confirmed) and across the width (weft direction, if confirmed). Pull each direction using the same agreed force or a consistent hand procedure, record the starting and extended lengths, release, and record recovery after the same rest period. Keep direction labels and the procedure with the result.

A hand-flex photo shows that a piece can bend; it does not establish warp or weft stretch percentages. A polyester base can have some give depending on its construction, but do not infer bidirectional stretch or recovery without measuring the actual sample. Set acceptable stretch and recovery ranges for your design before testing.

4. Treat water and vapor observations as separate checks

For a simple visual water check, label the face side, use the same agreed amount of water and contact time on each candidate, and inspect both the surface and reverse side. Record the setup, duration and any visible wet-through. The site’s waterproof testing record provides context for documenting a defined observation.

A visual water check is not a rated hydrostatic-head result. Droplets on the face or no visible wet-through during an informal check do not establish a pressure rating, seam performance or a laboratory pass. If a rated value matters, agree on the test method, conditioning, acceptance value and report scope for the exact material.

Similarly, a steam demonstration can show visible vapor behavior under that demonstration’s conditions, but it does not quantify moisture vapor transmission rate (MVTR). If breathability is a purchasing criterion, specify the relevant method and acceptance target, and request a result tied to the material construction and lot.

5. Inspect the face-side wipe-clean behavior

Apply a representative, agreed soil or liquid to the intended outer face. Record the material face, amount, dwell time, wiping material, number of passes and any visible residue, staining or surface change. Use the same procedure for every candidate. This fabric’s wipe-clean behavior is useful to check against your intended use; the result depends on the substance, finish and cleaning procedure.

6. Launder a cut piece and inspect the laminate

Before washing, photograph and label the sample. Follow the care conditions proposed for the finished product, recording detergent, water temperature, cycle, drying approach and number of cycles actually performed. Afterward, inspect for bubbling, peeling, delamination, distortion, changed handfeel, edge separation and changes in the agreed water check. State the wash protocol and acceptance rule in advance; a short sample review does not establish a universal wash-cycle life.

Move from a swatch to a 5 metre trial

Request a small swatch first to confirm construction, color, handfeel and whether the sample merits further checks. Ask whether the swatch can be cut from the same material reference or lot intended for the trial. Agree the specific test methods and thresholds that matter for your application, and keep dated photos and the completed worksheet with the sample ID.

If the swatch meets your written criteria, the published 5 metre MOQ is a practical trial quantity for checking cutting, sewing, finished-product behavior and planned care. Before purchase, reconfirm the exact construction, width, weight, price, availability and batch-specific details; these may depend on the selected material and order. Inspect the 5 metre trial against the approved swatch before treating it as your production reference.

For a project review, send your intended use, requested construction, target quantity and worksheet through Request a Quote. The discussion can then distinguish the current polyester/TPU reference from any separately requested polyester-elastane/PU construction.
