Wet bag decoration guide

Screen Printing on Custom Wet Bags

This guide covers the documented one-color screen-printed wet bag and the decisions a buyer should approve on artwork and sample before confirming production.

Product QC: MikeInspection reference: May 6, 2026Updated October 28, 20265 minute read

Documented print and bag configuration

One-color screen printing on a 20 × 25 cm wet bag with 160 g/m² outer fabric, transparent matte TPU 0.3 mm lining, one white snap and no zipper.

Start with the actual bag specification

Decoration should be quoted against a defined product version. This reference is a 20 × 25 cm bag with 160 g/m² outer fabric and transparent matte TPU lining, 0.3 mm thick. It has one white snap, no zipper, and one-color screen printing. Record all of these in the same RFQ so the sample and bulk quote refer to the intended bag, not merely a logo image.

The specified print method is screen printing in one color. It does not by itself define the exact ink shade, print dimensions, location, artwork handling, or acceptable visual variation. Submit clean, production-ready artwork and identify the color reference and placement. Ask the supplier to clarify any artwork preparation or setup charges and any limitations for fine lines or small type before approving the sample.

Review artwork and placement on a sample

Use a printed sample to check the artwork version, orientation, scale and position on the actual bag panel. Confirm the printed area does not conflict with seams, the opening, snap placement or the way the bag is handled. Check that the approved mark is legible at the chosen size and that the visible result matches the agreed color direction. A digital proof can confirm layout intent but cannot show the final physical print.

For a repeatable approval, note the artwork revision, bag material and color, print color reference, placement dimensions and the sample approval date. Keep the approved sample or clear record available for production comparison. Specify the review conditions with the supplier; do not assume an unprovided tolerance or ink formulation.

Include decoration in sample and bulk planning

The sample fee is $30 and is creditable toward a bulk order. The stated sample timeframe is 3–5 days. Confirm what the sample includes, whether it carries the final artwork and when the timeframe begins. The sample gives the buyer an opportunity to review the actual bag and print before bulk approval.

MOQ depends on the selected outer material: 1,000 pieces for polyester and 2,000 for canvas. Confirm the material route, color and whether the minimum applies separately to each artwork or version. Production capacity is 200,000 pieces per month, which is capacity information and not an order lead time. The stated carton quantity is 500 pieces; request packed carton dimensions and gross weight for the final version.

Buyer checklist for custom print

  • Confirm bag dimensions, outer material and TPU lining specification.
  • Record the single print color, artwork revision and color reference.
  • Define print size and placement relative to seams, opening and snap.
  • Approve a sample showing the actual print and product configuration.
  • Confirm material-specific MOQ, sample fee and sample schedule in writing.
  • Confirm carton count and packing facts for shipping estimates.

These are review criteria, not assertions of a particular durability result. No post-wash print result or performance test is claimed here. If your product plan requires a care-related print claim or a specific appearance after use, agree on an appropriate evaluation and acceptance criteria before ordering.

Send a complete print RFQ

Include quantity, polyester or canvas preference, bag color, dimensions, one-color artwork, desired placement, packaging and destination. Ask the supplier to return the proposed sample and production specification in writing. This makes it easier to compare the sample, approved artwork and quotation as one versioned item.

Frequently asked questions

How many print colors are included in this specification?

The documented option is one-color screen printing. Confirm ink color, artwork and placement in the written specification and sample approval.

Can a printed sample establish post-wash durability?

No post-wash durability result is provided here. If that matters, define a suitable care and evaluation method for the exact product and approve criteria before production.

What sample and MOQ details should I use?

The sample is $30, creditable toward bulk, and the stated timeframe is 3–5 days. MOQ is 1,000 for polyester and 2,000 for canvas. Confirm applicability to your version in the quote.
