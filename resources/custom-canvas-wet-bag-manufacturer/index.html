Custom Canvas Wet Bag Manufacturer: A Buyer’s Specification Guide

A useful custom brief turns a product idea into measurable decisions. This example records one reported canvas wet bag configuration, then shows what to confirm on the sample and in the order specification.

Example specification at a glance

Reported configuration: 20 × 25 cm, 160 g/m² outer fabric, 0.3 mm matte transparent TPU lining, one white snap and no zipper, with a single-color screen print. Reported canvas MOQ: 2,000 pieces. Treat these as the described specification and confirm the final tolerances, material identity and construction on the approved sample.

Write down the finished dimensions

Record whether 20 × 25 cm is the finished external size and how each side is measured, including whether the measurement is taken flat. Confirm seam allowance, opening position and any corner or gusset construction separately. Add agreed tolerances to the purchase specification; the nominal dimensions alone do not define acceptance.

Identify the outer fabric and lining

The reported outer fabric weight is 160 g/m². Confirm its fibre composition, color, surface and source for the exact order rather than relying on a generic label such as “canvas.” The reported lining is matte transparent TPU at 0.3 mm. Ask the supplier to identify the selected material in the written sample specification and note any approved substitutions.

A TPU lining description does not by itself establish how the finished bag contains liquid. If containment matters to the product claim, define the intended use and agree a check method, observation period and acceptance criteria for the complete bag, including seams and closure. Do not infer a test result from the material description or photos.

Specify the opening and closure

This example has one white snap and no zipper. Confirm snap material, color, placement and attachment on the sample. A single-snap closure is a design detail; it should not be presented as proof of leakproof or waterproof performance. Check that the opening works for the intended contents and handling.

Approve the print with a physical sample

The reported decoration is a single-color screen print. Supply vector artwork where available, identify the ink color and print dimensions, and mark the intended position from a named edge or seam. Review the print on the actual outer fabric for placement, legibility and appearance. Record the artwork revision and any changes before bulk production.

Plan MOQ, sampling and order quantities

The reported canvas MOQ is 2,000 pieces. A sample is reported at $30, creditable against a bulk order, with a sample period of 3–5 days. Confirm what the sample includes, when that period begins, shipping arrangements and how the credit is applied to your order.

Reported production capacity is 200,000 pieces per month. Capacity describes a production capability; it is not the promised completion time for a specific order. Request a project schedule based on approved materials, artwork, quantity and packaging. The reported packing quantity is 500 pieces per carton; confirm carton dimensions, gross/net weights, marks and whether the quantity applies to your chosen pack format.

Use the sample review to close open details

The reported quality record names Mike as QC reviewer and gives the inspection date as May 6, 2026. This records reviewer and date only; no test method or result is stated. For your own order, capture the sample identifier, review date, observations, requested changes and approval status. See the pouch specification guide for a broader checklist.

Prepare a clear request for quotation

  • Finished size, measurement points and tolerances
  • Outer fabric identity, composition, weight and color
  • TPU lining specification and finish
  • One white snap, no zipper, and closure placement
  • Single-color print artwork, color and location
  • Canvas quantity, sample scope and packing format
  • Carton count, shipping destination and project schedule
  • Any product-specific containment check and agreed criteria

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ for a custom canvas wet bag?

The reported MOQ for canvas wet bags is 2,000 pieces. Confirm it against the exact material, size, print and packaging brief.

What are the sample terms?

The reported sample charge is $30, creditable against a bulk order, with a sample period of 3–5 days. Confirm the start point and scope for the project.

Is 200,000 pieces per month an order lead time?

No. It is reported monthly production capacity. Ask for a separate project schedule for your order.

Does the TPU lining mean the finished bag is leakproof?

No performance result is established by the lining specification. Agree a suitable check for the complete approved bag before making a containment claim.
