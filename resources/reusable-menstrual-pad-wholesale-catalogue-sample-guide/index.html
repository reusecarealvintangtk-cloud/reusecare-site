Reusable Menstrual Pad Wholesale Catalogue: How to Compare Styles and Request Samples

Choose identifiable styles, compare the specifications that matter, and request samples tied to a written wholesale quote.

Prepared by ReuseCareNovember 27, 2026
Browse menstrual pad references →Request samples →

A wholesale catalogue is a starting point for choosing what to sample. For retailers and distributors, the most useful shortlist links each catalogue reference to a role in the assortment, a question the sample should answer, and the exact version the supplier will quote. That way, the style your team approves is the one the supplier prices for bulk production.

Shortlist by catalogue reference

Start in the menstrual pad wholesale catalogue and use the reusable menstrual pads overview for product context. Copy each model reference exactly as shown. If a listing has no visible reference, send its link and ask the supplier to confirm the model code. When a listing shows several variants, record the size or configuration you want rather than relying on the photo alone.

Choose up to three styles that answer different buying questions. One might be a candidate for your core range, another an alternative fit, and a third a possible addition if it offers something your first two choices do not. Compare the samples against the versions you can order

Ask the supplier to label each sample with its model reference and variant, and to identify any difference between the sample and the proposed bulk version. An available sample may help you assess shape or closure placement even if it uses a different fabric or finish. Record what it lets you approve and what still needs a production-representative sample.

Use the same checklist for every sample: measure length and width, check how the wings fasten, and compare seam finish and bulk when folded. If you wash the samples, use the same care routine for each and note any change in dimensions, shape, or closure function. Use the absorbency testing and wash durability guide to frame follow-up questions, and keep your own observations separate from supplier test results.

For material documentation, ask which exact layer, fabric, and variant each document covers. A document for the top fabric does not establish the properties of the absorbent core, backing, or finished pad. The materials and layers guide helps you name the layer you mean, while the guide to matching products with documents helps you check whether a report applies to the version being quoted.

Send a sample request the supplier can quote

Include your shortlist, sample quantities by reference, estimated bulk quantity by style, and any requested changes to materials, artwork, or packaging. Ask which samples are available as shown, which must be prepared, and whether a revised sample would be needed before bulk approval.

For a selected custom menstrual pad quote, the proposed route is 1,000 pieces, with indicative sample preparation of 3–7 days and bulk production of 25–35 days. These figures are specific to that quote route and subject to model, material, artwork, approval, and order confirmation. The quantity is not a catalogue-wide minimum, and the time ranges exclude transport. See the MOQ, sample cost, and lead time guide for details to confirm.

Request a menstrual pad sample quote with your completed shortlist. After review, confirm the approved sample reference and any agreed changes in writing before placing the bulk order.
