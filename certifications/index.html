Buyer verification

How to review a document.

Use the same checks for ReuseCare and any supplier under consideration.

01 / IDENTIFY

Match the holder

Check the legal entity, manufacturing site and address shown on the document.

02 / SCOPE

Match the product

Confirm that the tested article, material or management-system scope is relevant to your order.

03 / VALIDATE

Check status

Review the reference number, issue and expiry dates, issuing body and any public verification record.
