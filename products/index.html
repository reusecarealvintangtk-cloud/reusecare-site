Before you enquire.

Can I order as few as 5 pieces of a finished product?

Some basic styles start at 5 pieces. Basic-style minimums range from 5 to 300 pieces, depending on the selected product. Ask us to confirm the exact minimum.

Does every product ship within 2 days?

No. Only styles confirmed in stock can dispatch within 2 days. Custom printing, material changes and packaging have their own timing confirmation. Delivery time depends on destination and shipping method.

Can I choose any of your materials?

You can choose from the available material range. We confirm the material and construction for your product; this does not mean every combination is held in ready stock.

What is the minimum for custom printing?

For finished products, custom printing starts from 300 pieces. Fabric printing quantities are reviewed separately. Send the product, artwork and quantity so we can confirm how the minimum applies and quote sampling and production timing.

How is custom packaging quoted?

Packaging is reviewed case by case. Share the format, size, artwork and order quantity so we can confirm the minimum, cost and timing.
