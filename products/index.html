Before you enquire.

Can I order as few as 5 pieces?

Some basic styles start at 5 pieces. Basic-style minimums range from 5 to 300 pieces, depending on the selected style. Please ask us to confirm the exact minimum for your product.

Does every product ship within 2 days?

No. Only in-stock styles can dispatch within 2 days. Confirm the selected style and quantity with us first. Custom printing, material changes and packaging requirements need their own timing confirmation. Delivery time depends on the destination and shipping method.

Can I choose any of your materials?

Yes, you can choose from our full material range. We will confirm the material and construction for your product. This does not mean every material combination is available as ready stock.

What is the minimum for custom printing?

Custom printing starts from 300 pieces. Send the product, artwork and quantity so we can confirm how the minimum applies to your project and quote sampling and production timing.

How is custom packaging quoted?

Packaging is reviewed case by case. Share the format, size, artwork and order quantity so we can confirm the minimum, cost and timing.
