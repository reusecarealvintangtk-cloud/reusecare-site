Skin-contact layerCotton, bamboo-direction or polar-fleece references may be available by model. Confirm composition, fabric weight and finish.
Absorbent coreConfirm core material, layer count, dimensions and target use against the approved sample.
Waterproof layerFor PUL or TPU constructions, confirm base fabric, lamination or film detail and the specification used for that product.
Edges & fasteningRecord binding, stitching, snaps or other closure details for the selected style.
Finished dimensionsUse a dimension drawing or approved sample; do not infer size from a catalog image.

01

Dimension drawing

Length, width and product-specific fit points.

02

Layer sequence

Each layer in order, including core and waterproof backing.

03

Composition

Fiber or film composition and fabric weight where applicable.

04

Sample & packing

Approved sample code, pieces per pack and packaging format.

Published vs. confirmedPUL fabric has a published 150 cm / 120 g/m² / 5 metre specification. Other dimensions, weights, blends and layer counts are confirmed for the selected model and sample.
