Before you order.

Product-specific answers for your sourcing review.

What are the width, weight and minimum order?

150 cm width and 160 g/m² are published commercial references, and a 5-metre trial is an inquiry path. Confirm the exact batch width, weight and order details with your quote.

Does 5 metres also apply to custom printing?

The fabric minimum starts at 5 metres. A custom-printing minimum and sampling requirements are confirmed separately for your artwork.

Can I request a fabric sample?

Yes. Ask about a small evaluation sample and share the intended use. If it suits your needs, a 5-metre trial order can be discussed; availability and shipping are confirmed in the quote.

When can my fabric be dispatched?

Styles and quantities confirmed in stock can dispatch within 2 days. Custom printing and other production requirements have a separately confirmed timeline.
