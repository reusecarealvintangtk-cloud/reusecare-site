From fabric to finished order

A clear production journey

Use the approved sample and purchase specification as the reference at each stage. Confirm the checks and documentation needed for your particular order.

01 / Materials

Receiving & preparation

Review fabric composition, colors, components and material quantities before cutting. Identify the material lots assigned to the order.

02 / Workshop

Cutting & sewing

Follow the agreed dimensions and layer structure. Review stitching, assembly, snaps and trim placement against the reference sample.

03 / Quality control

Inspection checkpoints

Agree on appearance, measurement and workmanship checks. Define any functional testing and third-party inspection requirements before production.

04 / Packaging

Labels & retail packs

Confirm brand labels, care instructions, artwork and units per pack. Review barcodes and carton markings where required.

05 / Warehouse

Identification & storage

Keep finished-order quantities, product variants and carton identification clear. Confirm packing-list information before dispatch.

06 / Shipping

Dispatch & handover

Agree on the delivery terms, shipping method and required documents. Coordinate carton counts and handover details with the nominated logistics partner.

Quality documentation

Know what you are approving

Ask for the records relevant to your selected product and target market. Any test report or certificate should be checked for its scope, material, issuer and validity.
