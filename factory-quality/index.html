The production journey

Six stages, one clear approval trail.

The exact route depends on your product and customization level. The sequence below shows the main points where buyers normally confirm specifications, artwork, workmanship and packing details.

Product setup

Translate the brief into a workable product.

Start with the product style, material target, dimensions, construction, expected quantity and artwork. These inputs shape sampling, costing and the production route.

  • Product reference and target market
  • Material, layers and performance target
  • Logo, print and packaging files
Material preparation

Confirm color, print and material direction.

For custom programs, artwork and material details should be reviewed before cutting. Color expectations, print placement and material lots need a shared reference.

  • Artwork and color review
  • Fabric composition and lot identification
  • Print placement and usable width
Cutting

Prepare repeatable shapes and layer sets.

Patterns and cutting plans follow the agreed dimensions and construction. Components are grouped for the next assembly stage and checked against the order specification.

  • Pattern and dimension confirmation
  • Layer count and component grouping
  • Material-use planning
Sewing & assembly

Build to the approved construction.

Stitching, elastic, snaps, trims and label placement are reviewed against the reference sample. Any product-specific tolerance should be defined in the order brief.

  • Stitching and seam review
  • Snaps, elastic and trim placement
  • Brand and care-label position
Quality inspection

Check what matters for the approved order.

Inspection criteria can cover appearance, measurements, stitching, component placement and packing. Functional or third-party testing should be agreed before production.

  • Appearance and workmanship checks
  • Measurements and component placement
  • Order-specific inspection records
Packing & warehouse

Keep finished orders clear and ready for handover.

Pack quantities, labels, carton marks and shipment records should match the confirmed order. Shipping method, delivery terms and handover documents are finalized before dispatch.

  • Retail pack and carton confirmation
  • Variant and quantity identification
  • Packing list and logistics handover

Quality documentation

Know exactly what you are approving.

Ask for the records relevant to your selected product and target market. Any certificate or test report should be checked for its scope, material, issuer and validity.
