Product Development Notes

For B2B projects, the final construction should be confirmed around skin-contact material, absorbent core, waterproof layer, dimensions, target use and retail positioning. ReuseCare can structure the quotation once these requirements are defined.

Quality & Production

Incoming material checks, in-process sewing inspection and final product review should be aligned with the approved sample and purchase specification. Testing requirements depend on the product and destination market.

FAQ

Can I use my own logo and packaging?

Yes. ReuseCare is positioned for OEM and private label projects. Final options depend on the product, material, artwork and order quantity.

Can I request a sample before bulk production?

Yes. Sample timing and charges are confirmed after the specification and customization requirements are reviewed.

What information helps you quote faster?

Product style, expected quantity, target material, size, printing or logo requirements, packaging and destination country.
