What the product photos establish

The photos show the visible color, arrangement and, for one reference, a shaped/gusseted profile. They do not establish fiber percentages, inner-layer composition, absorbency, drying time, wash life, test results, certification, or universal pack quantity. Ask for a written layer map and evaluate the supplied sample against agreed criteria.

Turn a style into a purchase specification

For each candidate, record the style ID, intended diaper/pocket, declared composition by layer, total layers, finished length and width, measurement points and tolerance, edge or gusset construction, sample revision, and requested pieces per retail pack. Identify any required test method and acceptance limit rather than relying on a material name.

Sample checks before approval

  • Match the sample label and supplier declaration to the requested style and revision.
  • Measure length and width at agreed points; record actual values and deviations.
  • Inspect face, reverse, edges, seams and gusset shape against the approved drawing or reference.
  • If absorption or laundering matters, use a method, conditions and pass criteria agreed before testing; record observations without generalizing beyond that sample.
  • Approve one signed/dated reference and list unresolved changes before bulk production.

What the product photos establish

The photos show visible color, arrangement and, for one reference, a shaped/gusseted profile. They do not establish fiber percentages, inner-layer composition, absorbency, drying time, wash life, test results, certification or a universal pack quantity.

Purchase specification and sample checks

For each candidate record style ID, intended diaper/pocket, declared composition by layer, total layers, finished length and width, measurement points and tolerance, edge or gusset construction, sample revision, and pieces per retail pack. At receipt, match the label and declaration, measure against the agreed points, inspect faces, seams and shape against the reference, and record deviations. Agree functional or wash methods and pass criteria before testing, then tie observations to that sample revision.

Quote checklist

Send style reference, intended use, quantity, layer map, dimensions/tolerance, required checks, pack configuration, artwork or logo, packaging, requested documents and destination country. Ask the supplier to list open items and applicable commercial terms in the quote.

Product Development Notes

Confirm the skin-facing material, absorbent core, dimensions, target use and retail positioning in the final construction. A photo is a style reference; it cannot verify these product properties.

Quality & Production

Incoming material checks, in-process sewing inspection and final product review should be aligned with the approved sample and purchase specification. Testing requirements depend on the product and destination market.
