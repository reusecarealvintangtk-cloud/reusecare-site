What to include in your sample brief

Mark the gusset edges and seam positions you want reproduced. Request an inside-out sample and a construction drawing to compare the finish against your brief.

Checks for the beige construction reference

  • Agree the panel length, width and measurement points for each requested size; the photograph does not provide measurements.
  • Compare stitching, edge finish and seam bulk on the approved physical sample, including the reverse side that is not fully visible here.
  • Request a layer map and material declarations for the body and gusset. A white visible surface does not establish absorbency or waterproof performance.

Send your size chart, expected quantity by size and color, and any required test method with the sample request. ReuseCare can then quote the selected construction and packaging.
