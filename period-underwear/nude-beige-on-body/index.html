What to include in your sample brief

Mark your intended rise and coverage. Share target garment measurements and request a fitting on your own fit models before approving the pattern.

Fit decisions to confirm on a sample

  • Record waist, hip, rise and leg-opening measurements with tolerances for each proposed size.
  • Review waistband and leg elastic tension, coverage and movement on fit models from your target customer group.
  • Compare the worn view with front, back and inside-out sample photos. A single lifestyle image cannot verify sizing, absorbency or wash durability.

Provide a size chart and intended market in the RFQ. If a six-pair assortment is planned, state how sizes and colors should be split across the set.
