What to include in your sample brief

Annotate where a logo or care label should sit relative to the waistband and seams. Request matched front and back sample photos in your chosen color before approving artwork placement.

Front and back approval checklist

  • Supply a technical drawing with front and back rise, side seam and leg-opening measurement points.
  • Mark the placement and dimensions of the visible inner panel, labels and any branding relative to seams and trim.
  • Request both colorways in the same approved size, then compare construction and shade against physical standards.
