Turn a visual range into an order plan

  • List requested colors and quantities by size. The group image illustrates a range but does not confirm stock, a size range or six garments per retail pack.
  • Approve fabric swatches or lab dips under agreed lighting, then check the approved garment samples against the same standards.
  • Specify whether each pair is sold alone or in a mixed-color set, including labels, barcode plan and carton quantity.

Ask for a fit sample in the sizes you intend to sell and a quotation that separates garment, branding and packaging requirements.
