What to include in your inquiry

Fit and sizingShare your target fit, measurement chart, size range and size breakdown.
Materials and performanceSpecify fabric and gusset preferences, intended use, absorbency targets, test methods and any documentation your market requires. Confirm the construction and performance through sample review and agreed testing.
Colors and quantitiesList your preferred color references, estimated quantities by size and color, and destination country.
Branding and packagingSend artwork and preferences for labels, hangtags, retail packaging and assortment mix. Confirm feasible options and pack configuration with the quote.
Commercial requirementsShare your target launch date and ask for project-specific minimums, pricing and timing.

How to request a sample

  1. Reference the pictured style and share your size chart, target construction, performance requirements and branding brief.
  2. Review the proposed sample scope, charges and timing before sampling begins.
  3. Check fit, measurements, gusset construction and branding on the sample. Agree on any performance tests and approve the final specification before bulk production.
