What to include in your sample brief

Specify whether you want a light interior or a closer color match. Request physical swatches of the body and inner panel for approval together under the same lighting.

Checks for the rose color reference

  • Approve separate color standards for the outer body, binding and visible inner panel; screens and studio lighting cannot serve as color standards.
  • Confirm panel coverage, seam placement and the edge finish using a physical sample in the required size.
  • Request the exact fiber and layer declaration, plus agreed absorbency, wash and colorfastness tests if those claims matter for your market.

Include your target shade, size breakdown, branding artwork and pack quantity in the inquiry so the quote can distinguish color development from the base garment.
