Before we start

A few useful
buying questions.

Have a specific requirement?
Tell us about your project →

How is the minimum order quantity determined?

It depends on the selected product and its materials, printing and packaging requirements. Include your expected quantity in the inquiry so the options can be reviewed.

Can I customize the print, label and packaging?

Share your artwork or references along with your selected product. Material choices, label placement and packaging details can then be reviewed as part of the specification.

How do sampling and production work?

Begin with the product requirements. Confirm sample details, any revisions and the agreed production specification before proceeding with a bulk order. Timing is discussed for each project.

Which material and testing documents can I request?

Tell us the product, target market and documents you need. Availability and applicability should be confirmed for the specific material and product being quoted.
