How to use the liner

Lay one sheet flat on top of the nappy’s absorbent layer, next to the baby’s skin, to catch solids. At a nappy change, lift out the sheet and put it in the bin. Reusable cloth nappy inserts provide absorbency and are washed for reuse.

Material and test documents to request

  • Material declaration for the selected bamboo-based nonwoven option, including exact fibre composition, basis weight and construction.
  • Finished product specification and sample revision, including sheet dimensions, tolerances and pack count.
  • Any required wet handling or wet strength test documentation, with method, acceptance criteria and results tied to the proposed sample.
  • Any dispersibility report for the exact liner construction, plus a review of whether it supports the proposed disposal wording in the destination market.

Disposal guidance

Put used liners in the bin. Do not flush them down the toilet. Any flush-related wording on the reference packaging is pending technical documentation and destination-market review.

Sydney Water advises flushing only pee, poo and toilet paper, and points consumers to AS/NZS 5328:2022 when checking flushable claims for wipes. Read Sydney Water’s disposal guidance ↗
