Private-label specification checklist
Model & dimensionsRecord the exact style and the applicable length, width, rise, fit or opening measurements. Values are approved by sample.
Layer structureList the skin-contact layer, absorbent core and waterproof layer or backing in order.
Material compositionConfirm fiber, lamination and fabric-weight details from the selected material documents.
Artwork & brandingConfirm print repeat, color direction, logo placement, labels and any care-mark requirements.
PackagingConfirm pieces per pack, bag or box format, inserts, barcode requirements and carton marks.
Approval referenceUse the approved sample, artwork files and written specification as the shared production reference.
Ordering rules

How MOQ is confirmed.

The minimum depends on whether you are buying fabric, an existing finished style, custom printing or custom packaging.

Fabric

PUL from 5 metres

The published PUL specification is 150 cm wide and 160 g/m². Custom printing has a separately confirmed minimum.

Finished product

Basic styles: 5–300 pieces

The exact minimum depends on the product, selected style and confirmed stock availability.

Custom print

From 300 pieces

Confirm whether the minimum applies per artwork, size, color or style before sampling.

Custom packaging

Confirmed per project

MOQ and timing depend on packaging format, dimensions, artwork and total quantity.

Dispatch is not delivery.Confirmed in-stock finished styles can dispatch within 2 days. Sampling, custom production and transit are quoted separately.
