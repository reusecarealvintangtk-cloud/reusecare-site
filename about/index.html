About ReuseCare · Founded 2010

Reusable care & PUL manufacturing, shaped by care.

Founded in 2010 by Niki, a mother, and a professional nurse, ReuseCare makes reusable-care products and PUL fabrics for brands, wholesalers and distributors.

The beginning

Two perspectives.
One clear purpose.

Niki brought a mother’s perspective on everyday care. A professional nurse brought experience in care. Together, they founded ReuseCare in 2010.

Today, we make PUL and inner fabrics alongside reusable products for women, babies and everyday care. Start with an existing style or discuss materials, construction, printing, labels and packaging for a private-label range.

Mike, our Quality Control Lead, reviews sample sign-off. Each sample has a unique ID, recorded modifications and sign-off tied to the revision approved for bulk production. We agree product-specific test requirements and acceptance criteria as part of the brief.

Buyer questions

A few useful facts.

Who founded ReuseCare?

Niki and a professional nurse co-founded ReuseCare in 2010.

What products can I source?

PUL and inner fabrics, women’s care, baby products, makeup remover pads and wet bags. Browse all categories.

How does a product move toward bulk production?

Share a brief, review a sample and agree the specification. The published workflow records sample IDs, modifications and approval of the revision used for bulk.

How are PUL materials described?

Our PUL uses polyester or cotton base fabric laminated with TPU film. We agree construction and any required testing for each product.

Can I review compliance documents?

Yes. Tell Niki the product, material and destination market so the current documents and their scope can be checked.
