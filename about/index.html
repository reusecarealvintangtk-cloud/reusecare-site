Started with care.
Made for everyday reuse.

Founded by Niki, a mother, and a professional nurse, ReuseCare manufactures reusable menstrual care and cloth diaper products for brands, wholesalers and distributors.

The beginning

Two perspectives.
One clear purpose.

Niki brought her perspective as a mother. A professional nurse brought experience in care. Together they founded ReuseCare in 2010.

Today, our focus spans reusable menstrual pads and liners, cloth diapers, inserts and related accessories. Buyers can start from an existing style or work with us on materials, construction, printing, labels and packaging for a private label range.

The way we work begins with a clear product brief, a reviewed sample and an agreed specification before bulk production.

Inside the workflow

From your brief
to dispatch.

Every project needs its own specification. The stages below show how product requirements, quality checks and packing details can be defined together.

See our factory and quality process ↗
  1. 01

    Product brief

    Choose a style, intended use, target market and expected quantity.

  2. 02

    Materials & sample

    Review fabric options, layer structure, dimensions and a reference sample.

  3. 03

    Cutting & assembly

    Produce against the agreed pattern, construction and finishing details.

  4. 04

    Quality checkpoints

    Confirm appearance, measurements and any product-specific testing plan.

  5. 05

    Labels & packing

    Agree on brand labels, care details, packaging artwork and carton marks.

  6. 06

    Warehouse & dispatch

    Confirm variants, counts, shipping documents and handover requirements.
