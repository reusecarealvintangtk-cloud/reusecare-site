Certifications & market documentation

ReuseCare reports holding documentation covering OEKO-TEX®, CE, ISO, FDA-related requirements and CPC. Ask for current copies for the relevant product, material, manufacturing entity and destination market.

A document for one material, facility or model does not automatically cover every product. Certificate numbers, issuing bodies, standards, validity dates and covered models are not published in this overview; confirm them against the actual documents before making a claim.

OEKO-TEX®

Request the applicable certificate and check the material or article covered, certificate number and current validity. Do not assume a fabric certificate covers the finished product.

ISO

Ask for the exact ISO standard, certification body, legal entity, site scope and validity dates. ISO develops standards; independent certification bodies issue certificates.

CE

Request the applicable EU Declaration of Conformity and supporting documentation where the product is covered by CE-marking legislation. CE does not apply to every product category.

FDA-related records

Ask which registration, listing or other record applies to the specific product and entity. Establishment registration or device listing is not FDA approval, clearance or an FDA-issued certificate.

CPC

A Children’s Product Certificate is issued by the responsible manufacturer or importer for applicable children’s products. Request the certificate, covered models, applicable rules and supporting test reports.
